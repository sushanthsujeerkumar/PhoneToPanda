A GOOD FIRST CHECK

Two sensors.
One clear starting point.

  1. Identify each sensor.

    Connect your receiver and move one sensor at a time. Match the changing sensor ID to the upper arm or forearm.

  2. Align them on a table.

    Place both flat with their marked directions parallel. Start table alignment and keep them completely still.

  3. Capture a neutral pose.

    Secure the sensors to your right upper arm and forearm. Stand upright, relax your arm straight down and hold still for neutral capture.

  4. Move slowly, then record.

    Raise your arm and bend your elbow. Check that the correct segment moves. A fixed shoulder and measured arm lengths estimate wrist position; this is not independent position or finger tracking.
